FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Bajaj Finserv EMI works by dividing the total product cost into equal monthly installments over a chosen tenure (3 to 24 months). You pay a fixed amount every month via your existing bank account through NACH mandate. For No Cost EMI, the effective interest is zero — you pay only the product price spread across months.

For new Bajaj Finserv customers, you need: (1) Aadhaar Card (for identity & address proof), (2) PAN Card, (3) One cancelled cheque or bank passbook. Existing Bajaj Finserv customers may only need their registered mobile number and OTP. The entire process takes just a few minutes.

Yes! Bajaj Finserv EMI is available on smartphones, tablets, laptops, televisions, ACs, refrigerators, washing machines, coolers, microwave ovens, water purifiers, and most consumer electronics. No Cost EMI and Zero Down Payment options depend on brand tie-ups and current promotional offers.

For existing Bajaj Finserv customers, approval is instant — usually under 2 minutes. For new customers, the in-store process typically takes 5–10 minutes for document verification and NACH setup. Once approved, you can take your product home the same day with no waiting period.

Yes, Bajaj Finserv allows part-prepayment and foreclosure of your EMI loan. Foreclosure charges may apply depending on how many EMIs you have already paid. After paying at least 1 EMI, you can foreclose by visiting the Bajaj Finserv customer portal or calling their helpline. Pre-closing early saves you on remaining interest.

Generally, the minimum purchase amount for Bajaj Finserv EMI is ₹3,000. However, this can vary by product category and current promotions. Products above ₹5,000 typically have the widest range of EMI tenure options (3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 months). Visit our store for the most current limits.
